FAQ

Questions, answered simply

What is automatic writing?

Automatic writing is a form of channeling. Chittanando settles into a quiet, receptive meditative state and writes the guidance that comes through, rather than composing it with the thinking mind. In a Soul Speak session he writes in response to your questions and then reads and talks through the guidance with you.

Do I need to believe in anything or have experience?

No. You only need an open mind and a sincere question. Everything is explained in plain language, and you are welcome to approach the session with curiosity.

What happens in a session, and how do I prepare?

Sessions are one-to-one on a video call. Find a quiet, private space with a stable connection and headphones, and note down the questions or intention you want to bring. Soul Speak sessions run 45 minutes; Mind Mastery sessions run 60 minutes.

Who can I connect with?

People come to connect with their spirit guides, ascended masters and departed loved ones, or simply for clarity on a decision, relationship or pattern in their life. No specific outcome can be promised — guidance comes as it comes.

How do I pay?

There is no payment on this website. After you request a time, Chittanando confirms your booking personally and shares payment details. Sessions are US$100, or ₹8,500 for clients in India.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your session — just reply to your confirmation message. For changes within 24 hours, please get in touch and we will do our best to help.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. Sessions are spiritual guidance and meditation coaching. They do not diagnose or treat any condition and are not a substitute for care from a doctor, psychologist or other qualified professional.

How do Advanced Programs and couples work begin?

Advanced Programs and couples work are by application. Book a free 20-minute enquiry call so you can both see whether a program is the right fit; formats and fees are shared on application.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your details are used only to arrange your session, are never sold or shared for marketing, and what you share in a session stays between you and Chittanando.
