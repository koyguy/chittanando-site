About

Meet Chittanando

Chittanando (Ranjot Singh) is a spiritual medium and meditation coach. He received the link for Automatic Writing in 2005 from his Guru, Prof. Kashmira Elavia, trained for 13 years, and has worked with clients professionally since 2019.

Today his main work is private coaching for leaders and entrepreneurs. He combines body practice, mind training and ego work, so the people carrying the most responsibility can think clearly under pressure, stay well, and make room for the life outside work. No jargon, no gatekeeping — just honest guidance and practical tools.

“It’s here, in your body, on this Earth, in this very moment.”

FAQ

Questions, answered simply

Who is Leadership & Performance Coaching for?

Senior leaders, members of top management and entrepreneurs who want to sustain high performance without sacrificing their health, relationships or peace of mind.

What does “body, mind and ego work” mean in practice?

Body: breath, movement and Meipadam & Karlakattai practice for energy and a calmer stress response. Mind: meditation as mind training — focus, clarity and nervous-system regulation under pressure. Ego: recognising and reprogramming the habitual patterns behind overwork, conflict and self-sabotage. The three are worked on together, tailored to you.

How does it fit into a busy schedule?

Everything is one-to-one on video, so there is no travel. You choose times that suit you, and the practices you take away are short enough for a full calendar. The format of a programme is agreed with you after the discovery call.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Coaching is private and one-to-one. What you share stays between you and Chittanando, and your details are never sold or shared for marketing.

How do I start?

Book a free 20-minute discovery call. You will talk through what you want to change and see whether the programme is the right fit; format and fees are shared on application.

What is automatic writing?

Automatic writing is a form of channeling. Chittanando settles into a quiet, receptive meditative state and writes the guidance that comes through, rather than composing it with the thinking mind. In a Soul Speak session he writes in response to your questions and then reads and talks through the guidance with you.

Do I need to believe in anything or have experience?

No. The leadership work is practical and explained in plain language. For a Soul Speak session you only need an open mind and a sincere question.

How do I pay?

There is no payment on this website. After you request a time, Chittanando confirms personally and shares payment details. Single 1:1 sessions are US$100, or ₹8,500 for clients in India. Discovery calls are free.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your session — just reply to your confirmation message. For changes within 24 hours, please get in touch and we will do our best to help.

Do you work with couples?

Yes, couples sessions are also offered by enquiry. Book a discovery call and choose “Couples sessions”.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. Sessions are coaching, meditation training and spiritual guidance. They do not diagnose or treat any condition and are not a substitute for care from a doctor, psychologist or other qualified professional.
