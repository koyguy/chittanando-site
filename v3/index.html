FAQ

Questions I hear often

What is Soul Speak?

Soul Speak is a 45-minute session. I bring through guidance through Automatic Writing, and often through voice channeling, numerology and tarot, with energy transfer. We talk it through together. I share a recording afterwards. Each session is followed by a 15-minute grace period before the next one can begin.

Do I need to believe in anything?

No. You need a sincere question and enough curiosity to sit with what comes. I explain everything in plain language.

What if I'm sceptical?

That's okay. My job isn't to make you believe in anything. It's to help you experience your own truth.

What happens after Soul Speak?

The primary path is Awaken the Genius Within: eight two-hour sessions over three months, working with the inner child, shadow and ego, and your higher self. If you want a quieter weekly rhythm, Meditation is the gentler route. A year of Private Integration Coaching is available by application, for one client at a time.

Do you work with Indian clients?

Yes. Pricing in India is on request; write to me.

How do I pay?

There is no payment on this website. After you request a time, I confirm and share payment details by email or WhatsApp. International prices are listed in US dollars. India pricing is on request.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your session by replying to the confirmation. For changes inside 24 hours, write and I will do my best to help.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Sessions are private and one to one. What you share stays between us. I never sell your details or use them for marketing.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. This is spiritual guidance, meditation training and coaching. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it is not a substitute for care from a doctor, psychologist or other qualified professional.

The times in the calendar do not work for me.

The calendar currently shows Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 6:00 pm IST, with at least 24 hours' notice. If those times don't work, write with a few that do and we will find a slot.
