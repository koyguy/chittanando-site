Privacy

Privacy note

This plain-language note explains what happens to the details you share when you book.

What we collect

When you request a session we collect your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, country, your time zone, the session and time you chose, and anything you choose to write about your intention.

Why

Only to arrange and confirm your session, share payment details and the video-call link, and contact you about your booking.

Where it is kept

Booking details are stored in a private booking database accessible only to Chittanando. They are never sold, and never shared for marketing. This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

How long

Booking records are kept for as long as needed to provide sessions and meet record-keeping obligations, and then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to ig.permaheal@gmail.com.
