Privacy

Privacy note

This plain-language note explains what happens to the details you share when you request a session or a discovery call.

What we collect

When you send a request we receive your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, your time zone, the session and time you chose, and anything you choose to write about your intention. If you arrived through a poster, a QR code or a campaign link, tags such as src or UTM parameters from that link may be included in the email so we can see which page or poster you came from.

Why

Only to arrange and confirm your session, share payment details and the video-call link, and (if a campaign tag is present) to understand which public notice or page brought you here. There is no advertising, no tracking cookie, and no third-party analytics on this site.

Where it is kept

Requests arrive by email. Campaign tags may also sit temporarily in your browser’s session storage so they are not lost if you move between v2 pages. They are not sent to any analytics service. Details are never sold, and never shared for marketing.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to ig.permaheal@gmail.com.
