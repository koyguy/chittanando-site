About

Meet Chittanando

Chittanando (Ranjot Singh) is a spiritual medium and meditation coach. He received the link for Automatic Writing in 2005 from his Guru, Prof. Kashmira Elavia, trained for 13 years, and has worked with clients professionally since 2019.

He is a Certified Yoga Teacher from Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Delhi (2016–17), and a Certified Meipadam & Karlakattai Coach, trained under Guru Jothi Silambam in Pondicherry. Meipadam and Karlakattai belong to that Tamil martial lineage.

The work is built to move people from fragmentation to integration: channeling and lineage wisdom, mind training and shadow work, and somatic practice in one held process. Spiritual growth is not halwa. It asks for honesty, practice, and a body that can hold what opens.

“It’s here, in your body, on this Earth, in this very moment.”

Philosophy

How the work is held

The aim is simple: demystify spirituality, and help high-achievers and conscious seekers embody clarity, purpose and their own potential in ordinary life.

  • Authenticity

    Lineage and lived practice, spoken in plain language. No costume of holiness, and no promise that visualisation alone will do the work.

  • Integration

    Channeling, mind training and body work belong together. Insight that never lands in the body or in daily life does not last.

  • Embodiment

    The body has to be able to hold a higher charge. Breath, movement and martial-lineage practice are part of the path, not an afterthought.

  • Warrior presence

    Calm under intensity. The capacity to stay present when work, relationships or inner material get hard.

FAQ

Questions, answered simply

What is Soul Speak?

Soul Speak (Atmananda) is a 45-minute Automatic Writing session. Chittanando settles into a quiet, receptive state and writes the guidance that comes through, then reads and talks it through with you. A recording is shared afterwards. Each session is followed by a 15-minute grace period before the next one can begin.

Do I need to believe in anything?

No. You need a sincere question and enough curiosity to sit with what comes. Everything is explained in plain language.

What happens after Soul Speak?

The primary path is Awaken the Genius Within: eight two-hour sessions over three months, working with shadow, the inner child, the ego, and the higher self. If you want a quieter weekly rhythm, the meditation path (Dhyanananda) is the secondary route. A 12-month Private Integration Coaching year is available by application, for one client at a time.

How do I pay?

There is no payment on this website. After you request a time, confirmation and payment details are sent by email or WhatsApp. International prices are listed in US dollars. India pricing is available on request.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your session by replying to the confirmation. For changes inside 24 hours, write and we will do our best to help.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Sessions are private and one to one. What you share stays between you and Chittanando. Details are never sold or used for marketing.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. This is spiritual guidance, meditation training and coaching. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it is not a substitute for care from a doctor, psychologist or other qualified professional.

The times in the calendar do not work for me.

The 3:00–4:30 pm IST window is a starting example and can be adjusted. Write with a few times that work in your zone and we will find a slot.
