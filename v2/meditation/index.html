Dhyanananda

Meditation as training, not only relaxation.

Mind Mastery is a weekly hour of breath, stillness and attention, held long enough for the nervous system to change its habits. It is the quieter path after Soul Speak, if a three-month programme of shadow work is not where you want to start.

  • 1 hour a week
  • Minimum 3 months · 12 sessions
  • US$200 per session · US$2,400 for the container

What you are committing to

One 60-minute session each week, for at least three months (12 sessions). Recordings are shared, so you can practise between meetings. The price is US$200 a session, US$2,400 for the twelve. India pricing is available on request.

If you later want to step into Awaken the Genius Within or the year of private coaching, this work is a solid foundation rather than a detour.
