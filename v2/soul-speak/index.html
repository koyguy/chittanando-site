Book a Soul Speak What to expect

What happens in the session

Automatic Writing is a form of channeling. Chittanando settles into a quiet, receptive meditative state and writes what comes through, rather than composing it with the thinking mind. He then reads the writing with you and talks it through, so you can ask, test and take something usable into the week ahead.

People come to connect with spirit guides, ascended masters or departed loved ones, or simply for clarity on a decision, a relationship or a pattern. No specific outcome can be promised. Guidance comes as it comes. The invitation is to experience it, not to take it on faith.

The 15-minute grace period

Each Soul Speak is 45 minutes. A 15-minute grace period follows, and only then can the next 45-minute session begin. This keeps the work unhurried, for you and for the person who may be next.

How to prepare

Find a quiet, private room, use headphones, and write down the question you actually care about. A stable connection is enough. You do not need prior experience.

A few practical questions

Is a recording included?

Yes. A recording of the session is shared with you afterwards.

Can I book two sessions close together?

The next Soul Speak can begin only after the 15-minute grace period. In the current daily window that usually means one session in that window, unless the window itself is adjusted for you.

What if I am in another time zone?

The calendar shows times in your zone, with IST alongside. If the daily window does not work, write with a few alternatives.

What comes after this?

Many people go on to Awaken the Genius Within, the primary path after Soul Speak. Weekly meditation is a quieter alternative. A year of Private Integration Coaching is by application.
