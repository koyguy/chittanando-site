What this work looks at

A great deal of adult life is still being run by patterns laid down in the first seven years. This programme is a structured way to see that programming, work with the inner child and the ego, and practise contact with a clearer, more adult intelligence in yourself.

It is not mass-market manifestation, and it is not positive thinking. It is close, honest work, held over three months, with recordings of the live sessions so you can return to the material.

Shape of the three months

  • Month 1 · four two-hour sessions

    The opening stretch is denser, so the ground can be covered while the work is fresh.

  • Months 2 and 3 · two sessions a month

    The pace eases so the work can settle into ordinary life, rather than staying as an intense workshop high.

  • Included

    Two complimentary Soul Speak sessions, and live recordings of the work.
